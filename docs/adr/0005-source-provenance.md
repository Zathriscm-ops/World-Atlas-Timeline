# ADR 0005: Claims carry versioned evidence
Status: Accepted, M005, 2026-09-14.

Use independent Source, Citation, Claim, Dataset, Contributor and Revision records as specified in SOURCE_PROVENANCE.md. Entity-level bibliography alone cannot establish which date or boundary is supported. Preserve contradictory claims and immutable raw evidence. AI assistance is recorded as contribution, not evidence.

The representative audit satisfies DEP-05's feasibility investigation. DEP-04's acquisition procedure is defined; actual reviewed political geometry remains an explicit M028 prerequisite. This decision does not waive historical or rights review and does not implement M036/M037 early.
